## Chapter 3.1: Customer Segments, Value Propositions, Channels & Customer Relationships

Image (LMS): https://login.skillizee.io/s/articles/6aed510a7a40ce4170373917/images/image-20261106093127-1.png — saved locally as `public/chapter-3-1/lms-1.png` (1920×1080).

**Objective**
Understand who Eat Better serves, what promise it makes, how it reaches customers and how it keeps them.

**Customer Segments**
- Health-conscious families.
- Young professionals and students looking for better snacks.
- Gift buyers (festivals and personal gifting).
- Corporate buyers (employee gifting and events).

**Value Propositions**
- Familiar Indian taste, made with cleaner ingredients.
- No palm oil, refined sugar or bad fats (brand claim).
- 6-layer quality check and FSSAI certification.
- Convenience: order online or on quick-commerce apps.
- Ready-made gifting options.

**Channels**
- Own website (eatbetterco.com).
- Marketplaces such as Amazon.
- Quick-commerce apps: Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart.
- Corporate gifting and bulk order requests.

**Customer Relationships**
- Reviews and ratings, customer care by email, and a subscriber community.
- Content, offers and repeat-purchase combos.
- Transparency through kitchen visibility and the origin-story video.

**Key takeaway**
The left side of the canvas is about people: who they are, what they need and how you stay in touch.

**Think & discuss**
- Pick one customer segment and write what they want in one sentence.
- Which channel is best for first-time buyers? Which is best for loyal buyers?

## Chapter 3.2: Key Resources, Key Activities & Key Partners

**Objective**
Identify what Eat Better needs, does and depends on to deliver its promise.

**Key Resources**
- Its 50,000 sq. ft. kitchen and production facility.
- Recipes and product know-how.
- The Eat Better brand and customer trust.
- Its team (business databases list roughly 70 employees).
- Its website and online selling platform.

**Key Activities**
- Sourcing ingredients and running quality checks.
- Cooking in small batches and testing every batch.
- Packaging and order fulfilment.
- Marketing, community building and customer care.
- Developing new products and festive gifting ranges.

**Key Partners**
- Ingredient suppliers.
- Marketplaces and quick-commerce platforms.
- Logistics and delivery partners.
- Investors (Java Capital, Mumbai Angels, Prath Ventures, Spring Marketing Capital and others).
- Corporate clients for gifting and bulk orders.

**Key takeaway**
The middle and right of the canvas show what the company must be good at, and who it needs on its side.

**Think & discuss**
- What would happen to the brand if one key supplier failed?
- Which key activity do you think matters most for trust?
